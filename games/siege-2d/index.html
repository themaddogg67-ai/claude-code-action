HOW TO PLAY

💻 Keyboard & mouse

  • WASD — move
  • MOUSE — aim
  • CLICK (hold) — fire
  • R — reload
  • E — operator gadget
  • F — breach a wooden wall you face; hold on a bomb site to plant
  • H — open/close this help

📱 Touch (phone / tablet)

  • Left stick — move
  • Right stick — aim & auto-fire
  • R button — reload
  • E button — gadget
  • F button — breach wall / hold on a site to plant
  • Best played in landscape.

🎯 Goal (1 player)

You're the attacker. Storm the building and either eliminate all defenders (red) or reach bomb site A/B — follow the gold arrow & dashed line — and hold F to plant the defuser, then survive the countdown. A red arrow points to any defender shooting at you.

🎮 2 Players · 1v1 duel (same keyboard)

Two humans, one keyboard, no AI. You face where you walk and shoot forward. Last one standing wins; if time runs out the higher health wins.

  • Player 1 — WASD move · SPACE shoot
  • Player 2 — ↑←↓→ move · ENTER shoot
Objective: reach a bomb site (A or B, follow the gold arrow), hold F to plant & arm the defuser — then survive. Or just eliminate every defender.
